Report

Write 20 short sentences in Italian about the person you interviewed. Use the third person (lui/lei), not "I". Then present your report to the class or record it (1–2 minutes).

One answer = one sentence. You chose 20 questions, so you have 20 answers. Keep each sentence short. If an answer is long, make it simple: one idea per sentence.

From "I" to lui/lei: how to change their words

People answer about themselves ("I lived…"). Your report is about them ("She lived…"). Change the verb and the words that show whose it is.

She says: Io abitavo a Napoli. → You write: Mia nonna abitava a Napoli.
He says: Sono nato nel 1950. → You write: Mio nonno è nato nel 1950.
She says: La mia casa era piccola. → You write: La sua casa era piccola.

Gender: a grandmother is nata, andata, piccola. A grandfather is nato, andato, piccolo. Choose one and keep it.

Present or record

Your presentation is 1–2 minutes. You are graded on your own pronunciation and fluency, so use your own voice.

  • Read your report out loud two or three times first. With short sentences, 20 sentences fit in about two minutes.
  • Speak slowly. Say each word clearly.
  • Find a quiet spot. If you record, hold the phone steady and record only yourself.
  • Do not say addresses or private details.
