Imperfetto or passato prossimo?

These sentences sound like things your person might tell you. For each one, pick the tense, then type the verb form. Accents count.

Clue words

Imperfetto: habit or description

da piccolo/a · sempre · di solito · ogni estate · ogni giorno · la domenica · quando era giovane

Think: how things were, or what they used to do again and again.

Passato prossimo: one finished event

nel 1965 (a date) · un giorno · per la prima volta · a diciotto anni

Think: one thing that happened and was finished.

Careful with "una volta": "una volta" can mean "one time" (passato prossimo) or "back then" (imperfetto). Use the other clues to decide.
