Rubric

How you are graded

50 points. Five categories, 10 points each. Each category has four levels: 10, 8, 6, or 3.

Questions 10 points · Step 1 · the 20 graded questions; warm-ups are not graded

10Excellent
All 20 graded questions use the tu form correctly, with correct question words and a good variety of questions.
8Good
Mostly correct tu form and question words, with a few errors or little variety.
6Developing
Several errors in the tu form or the question words, and most questions are alike.
3Beginning
Many questions are not in the tu form or use the wrong question word, or the list is incomplete.

Imperfetto 10 points · Step 3 report

10Excellent
The imperfetto is used correctly for habits and descriptions throughout, with correct endings.
8Good
Mostly correct, with one or two errors in endings or in choosing the imperfetto.
6Developing
Some correct uses, but several errors in endings or in knowing when to use it.
3Beginning
Rarely correct, or the imperfetto is missing.

Passato prossimo 10 points · Step 3 report

10Excellent
The passato prossimo is used correctly for single events, with the right helper (essere or avere) and the right agreement.
8Good
Mostly correct, with one or two errors in the helper, the agreement, or the participle.
6Developing
Some correct uses, but several errors in the helper, the agreement, or the participle.
3Beginning
Rarely correct, or the passato prossimo is missing.

Content and organization 10 points · Step 3 report

10Excellent
20 sentences in the third person, clearly organized, with specific details from the interview.
8Good
20 sentences, mostly in the third person and organized, with some general details.
6Developing
Fewer than 20 sentences, some first person, or an order that is hard to follow.
3Beginning
Far too short, mostly first person, or little connection to the interview.

Presentation 10 points · Step 3 presentation or recording

10Excellent
Clear pronunciation and smooth delivery, 1–2 minutes, and easy to understand.
8Good
Mostly clear, with a few pronunciation errors or pauses.
6Developing
Understandable with effort, with frequent pauses or pronunciation errors.
3Beginning
Hard to understand, too short, or not finished.

Accents count

Accents are part of the spelling, so a missing or wrong accent is an error. When your teacher marks one wrong, you will see why.

è (is / has) · e (and)
perché (why, because)
città (city)
più (more)
così (like this, so)
