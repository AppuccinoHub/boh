Italiano 3 · Summative project · 50 points

Intervista a un parente più grande (come una nonna o un nonno)

Interview with an Elder · a grandparent, an older relative, or an older family friend

Interview an older person you know about their childhood and their life, then report back in Italian. The interview can be in English or in Italian. Everything you write and present is in Italian.

Before you begin

  1. Choose your person: a grandparent, an older relative, or an older family friend you know well.
  2. Ask permission: tell them about the project, and that they can skip any question.
  3. Pick your track (below).

Pick your track

Both tracks use the same questions, the same steps, and the same rubric.

What you turn in

  1. Your list of 20 graded questions, plus up to 5 warm-up questions (Step 1)
  2. Your interview notes (Step 2)
  3. One photo or drawing of something from your person's past (Step 2)
  4. Your report: 20 short sentences in Italian (Step 3)
  5. Your presentation or recording, 1–2 minutes (Step 3)

Your lesson path

Do the stages in order. Stages 1 to 4 are practice. You do not turn them in. Stages 5 to 7 are turned in.

Rules for the whole project

  • Everything you write and present is in Italian. Your interview and notes can be in English.
  • Accents count: è, perché, città, più, così.
  • See the rubric for exactly how you are graded.

Privacy: share only what the person is comfortable sharing, and leave out addresses and personal details. Ask permission before you take a photo or make a recording.
