Questions

Choose 15 graded questions from the bank of 80, including at least 4 you write yourself. You need at least 8 in the imperfetto and at least 4 in the passato prossimo. You may also add up to 5 warm-up questions from group 1 (easy starters). Warm-ups are not graded. Tap a question to add it. Tap it again to remove it.

Start with the green "quick answer" questions. They can be answered with one word, a number, a place, a year, or yes/no. That makes your interview and your report easier.

How to ask in the tu form

You are talking to a person you know well, so you use tu (informal you). With tu, the verb usually ends in -i.

Dove abiti? · Che cosa facevi? · Dove sei nato/a? · Quanti anni hai?
la tua casa · il tuo lavoro · tua madre · ti piace

Question words: dove (where), quando (when), come (how), che cosa (what), chi (who), quanti/e (how many), perché (why), qual è / qual era (which, what is / was).

Write your own question

Write at least 5 of your own. Use the tu form. Short questions with a short answer are best. Then tell the page which tense it uses.
